📄 InvoiceGen for monday.com

Generate professional PDF invoices from your board items in 3 steps.

1

Go to Settings and add your company info

Enter your company name, address, tax rate, and currency. This info appears on every invoice you generate.

2

Select your board and map columns

Choose which board to invoice from, then map your Description, Quantity, and Rate columns. InvoiceGen will automatically calculate totals.

3

Enter client details and generate PDF

Add the client name, email, and due date. Click "Generate Invoice" to download a professional PDF instantly.
